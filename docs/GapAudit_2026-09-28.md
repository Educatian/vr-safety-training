# Competent Person: gap audit (revamp/v2, 2026-09-28)

Scope: read-only audit of `C:\Users\jewoo\GameDev\vr-safety-training` (HEAD f2b572f plus uncommitted Minimap/NameTag work). I read docs/GDD, TechSpec, Tasks, TestPlan, SiteLayout, Characters, AssetManifest and AssetBudget; all `_Game/Scripts/{Core,Runtime,Editor}`; the `_Game/Tests`; and captures in t_episodes, t_gate, t_monday_play, t_tuesday and t_wednesday.

I judged it against two bars:
- **(1) Shippable.** A university instructor could assign it as a web game.
- **(2) Evidence-based training quality.** That means ECD/assessment validity, feedback and transfer.

Items already in progress are excluded: EP4/EP5 content, the Tripo NPC swap, name tags, the minimap, the first WebGL build and proxy deployment.

Severity: **Blocker** means you cannot assign it to a class. **High** means it undermines learning or validity, or is visibly broken. **Med** means it is expected in a complete game. **Low** is polish.
Effort: S ≤ 1 day, M 2–5 days, L > 1 week.
