# OSHA alignment boundary

This simulation is authored as OSHA-aligned construction safety practice. It is not an OSHA card, legal compliance certification, or a substitute for the employer's written programs, site procedures, competent person determinations, or qualified-person authorization.

## Project rule

Every inspectable condition has an `OshaScenarioCatalog` entry with a current 29 CFR Part 1926 citation, the role authorized to make the relevant decision, and an observable learner action. The EditMode catalog tests fail if an authored inspection target is not traceable.

## Current construction-aligned mappings

| Training area | OSHA source | Learner evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall protection and guardrails | 29 CFR 1926.501 and 1926.502 | Stop access, recognize a missing control, and request approved protection. |
| Housekeeping and material storage | 29 CFR 1926.25 and 1926.250 | Clear travel routes and place materials in a stable designated area. |
| Signs and boundaries | 29 CFR 1926.200 | Preserve work-zone isolation and report compromised controls. |
| Fire protection and egress | 29 CFR 1926.150 and 1926.34 | Maintain extinguisher access and clear egress. |
| Hazard communication | 29 CFR 1926.59 | Identify missing labels, stop handling, and follow the written site procedure. |
| Electrical work | 29 CFR 1926.416 and 1926.417 | Stop work and escalate isolation/lockout verification to a qualified person. |

## Release gate

Before release, an EHS reviewer must check each scenario against the active federal standard, any applicable State Plan, task-specific employer procedures, and actual worksite conditions. An OSHA or scenario-content change requires a new review date and approval. The app must not present an in-app score as regulatory certification.

Official sources: [29 CFR Part 1926](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/), [Fall protection](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926SubpartM), [Material storage](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.250), [Fire protection](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.150), [Hazard communication](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.59), and [Lockout and tagging of circuits](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.417).
